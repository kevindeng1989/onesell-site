Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

OneSell ERP (“OneSell”, “we”) is operated by Dongguan Chaojicheng E-commerce Co., Ltd. (东莞市超级橙电子商务有限公司). This policy explains what data we handle when a seller uses OneSell at onesellerp.com, including when a seller connects a TikTok Shop store.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: company name, usernames, hashed passwords, roles and operation logs of the seller’s team members.
  • Store authorization data: shop ID, shop name, region and the access / refresh tokens issued by TikTok Shop when the seller authorizes OneSell. Tokens are stored encrypted.
  • Store business data obtained through the TikTok Shop API, only within the scopes the seller authorized: products and SKUs, orders and order items, fulfillment and logistics tracking, returns and refunds, promotions, and settlement statements. Order records may contain buyer information that TikTok Shop returns (for example buyer nickname and buyer messages).
  • Content the seller creates or uploads: sourced product information, images, videos, templates, purchase prices and notes.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and server logs needed for security and troubleshooting.

2. How we use data

  • To provide the ERP functions the seller asks for: listing and optimizing products, processing and fulfilling orders, handling after-sales, tracking logistics, and reporting sales and profit.
  • To run AI features the seller starts (copywriting, image and video generation, listing diagnosis).
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix faults.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use buyer data for advertising or for any purpose other than serving the seller who authorized the store.

3. Sharing

  • TikTok Shop: we send data back to TikTok Shop only to carry out actions the seller requests (for example publishing a product or arranging shipment).
  • AI and infrastructure providers: when the seller uses an AI feature, the product text and images involved are sent to our model providers (Volcengine, Alibaba Cloud Model Studio, Agnes AI) to generate the result. Our servers and storage are hosted with a cloud infrastructure provider. These providers process data on our instructions only.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose data when required by law.

4. Storage and security

  • Each company’s data is kept in its own isolated database area; one company cannot read another’s data.
  • Connections use HTTPS. Store tokens and third-party keys are encrypted at rest. Passwords are stored as salted hashes.
  • Access to production systems is limited to authorized staff.

5. Retention and deletion

  • We keep data while the seller’s account is active.
  • When a seller removes a store authorization, uninstalls OneSell from TikTok Shop, or asks us to close the account, we stop syncing that store and delete its tokens. Store business data is deleted within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.
  • To request deletion or a copy of your data, email dev@onesellerp.com from the account’s contact address. We respond within 15 days.

6. Your rights

Sellers and their team members can access and correct account data inside OneSell, withdraw a store authorization at any time in Settings → Shop authorization or in TikTok Shop Seller Center, and ask us to delete data as described above. Buyers who want to exercise rights over their order data should contact the seller or TikTok Shop; we will assist the seller in responding.

7. Cookies

OneSell uses browser local storage only to keep you signed in and remember interface preferences. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

8. Changes and contact

We will post changes on this page and update the date above. Questions: dev@onesellerp.com.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 23 日

万售ERP（OneSell ERP，下称“我们”）由东莞市超级橙电子商务有限公司运营。本政策说明卖家使用 onesellerp.com（包括授权 TikTok Shop 店铺）时，我们处理哪些数据。

一、我们收集的数据

  • 账号数据：企业名称、成员用户名、加盐哈希后的密码、角色和操作记录。
  • 店铺授权数据：店铺 ID、店铺名称、站点，以及卖家授权时 TikTok Shop 签发的访问令牌和刷新令牌。令牌加密保存。
  • 通过 TikTok Shop 接口取得的店铺经营数据（仅限卖家授权的权限范围）：商品和 SKU、订单和订单明细、履约和物流轨迹、退货退款、促销活动、结算账单。订单记录可能包含 TikTok Shop 返回的买家信息（如买家昵称、买家留言）。
  • 卖家自己创建或上传的内容：货源信息、图片、视频、模板、采购价和备注。
  • 技术数据：IP 地址、浏览器类型和服务器日志，用于安全和排查故障。

二、数据用途

  • 提供卖家需要的 ERP 功能：商品上架和优化、订单处理和发货、售后、物流追踪、销售和利润统计。
  • 执行卖家发起的 AI 功能（文案、图片和视频生成、商品诊断）。
  • 保障服务安全、防止滥用、修复故障。

我们不出售个人数据，不把买家数据用于广告，也不用于为授权卖家提供服务以外的任何目的。

三、数据共享

  • TikTok Shop：只有在执行卖家发起的操作（如发布商品、安排发货）时，才向 TikTok Shop 回传数据。
  • AI 与基础设施服务商：卖家使用 AI 功能时，相关的商品文字和图片会发送给模型服务商（火山引擎、阿里云百炼、Agnes AI）生成结果。服务器和存储托管在云服务商。这些服务商只按我们的指示处理数据。
  • 法律要求：法律要求时我们可能披露数据。

四、存储与安全

  • 每家企业的数据放在各自独立的数据库区域，企业之间互相读不到。
  • 全程 HTTPS；店铺令牌和第三方密钥加密存储；密码以加盐哈希保存。
  • 只有获授权的人员可以访问生产系统。

五、保存期限与删除

  • 账号有效期间我们保存数据。
  • 卖家解除店铺授权、在 TikTok Shop 卸载万售ERP，或要求注销账号后，我们停止同步该店铺并删除其令牌；店铺经营数据在 30 天内删除，法律另有要求的除外。
  • 如需删除数据或索取数据副本，请用账号联系邮箱发信至 dev@onesellerp.com，我们在 15 天内答复。

六、你的权利

卖家及其成员可以在系统内查看和更正账号数据，随时在“设置 → 店铺授权”或 TikTok Shop 卖家中心解除授权，并按上文要求我们删除数据。买家如需就订单数据行使权利，请联系卖家或 TikTok Shop，我们会协助卖家处理。

七、Cookie

我们只用浏览器本地存储来保持登录状态和记住界面偏好，不使用广告或跨站追踪 Cookie。

八、变更与联系

政策变更会发布在本页并更新上方日期。联系邮箱：dev@onesellerp.com。
